Why we exist

Most organisations don’t struggle with AI because the technology isn’t ready. They struggle because the projects never ship — endless discovery, a proof-of-concept that stalls, a roadmap measured in years. Meanwhile the real problem keeps growing: knowledge scattered across documents, inboxes and the heads of busy people, the same questions asked every week, and experience that quietly walks out the door every time someone resigns.

Neomeric was founded in 2015 to close that gap. We’re an AI product studio, not a deck factory: we design, build and operate AI products end-to-end, and we hold ourselves to a delivery standard — a working pilot in four to six weeks, production in months, not years. That discipline is the whole point of “AI Simplified”.

What we do

We work in two ways. First, custom AI builds — bespoke products designed around a client’s own roadmap, shipped end-to-end and then run for them. Second, our own productised line, where we take what we’ve learned across dozens of builds and turn it into software any organisation can switch on. NeoMind is the leading product in that line.

Over the years that’s meant 7+ products shipped across 4+ industries — finance, logistics, services and manufacturing. Alongside NeoMind, the studio has built Aourix, a lead-to-cash ERP for manufacturers, and AuraFusen, an HR platform — plus custom systems for clients. Different problems, one operating habit: build the smallest thing that’s genuinely useful, get it live, then compound it.

NeoMind — our AI workforce platform

Turn the knowledge a business already has into employees it can rely on — grounded in its own information, taking real actions, and measurable like any member of staff.

NeoMind treats AI the way you treat people: give it your knowledge, give it a job, and hold it to a standard. Everything runs on a single Brain — one place your organisation’s knowledge lives — with a set of digital employees that all draw from it. Change a fact once in the Brain, and every employee is instantly up to date. And because an AI you can’t measure is an AI you can’t trust with real work, every employee can be given a business objective and weighted KPIs, with each conversation scored against that rubric — guardrail violations score negative, so the number can’t be gamed.

Hugo is the one we’re proudest of. Customer-facing AI is becoming common; an AI that remembers how your company actually works — and answers your own team instantly — is the part that compounds. It’s the difference between a smarter website and an organisation that stops losing its memory.

How we think about the work

We hold a clear bright line: a NeoMind employee does the routine work brilliantly and at volume — answering, capturing, booking, deflecting — but it never signs, commits, or makes a binding promise on your behalf. When a conversation reaches a judgement call, the right move is to escalate to a human, and the scoring rewards exactly that. We think this honesty is what makes a measurable AI employee safe to actually deploy.

And your knowledge stays yours. NeoMind learns from the sources you give it to serve you; it isn’t a way for your data to train someone else’s model. Trust is the product.
