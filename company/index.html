Why NeoMind exists

Most businesses don’t have an AI problem.
They have a knowledge problem.

Customers wait too long for answers.
Staff ask the same questions every week.
Know-how sits buried in documents — and walks out the door when experienced people leave.
And the owners who do try AI can’t see whether it’s actually helping.

We built NeoMind to change that.

Every resignation is a small fire. The person who knew how the quoting really works, which customers need careful handling, why the process is the way it is — gone in four weeks, and the knowledge with them. Most businesses run on memory that lives in people’s heads, and they only find out how much when someone leaves.

We think that’s the real problem AI should be solving. Not making a business sound smarter — making sure what it knows survives, and works, without depending on any one person’s memory.

Our vision
Every company will have digital employees. The ones that win will be able to trust them — and measure them.

What NeoMind is

NeoMind turns the knowledge a business already has into AI employees it can manage and measure like staff. Everything runs on a single Brain — one place the organisation’s knowledge lives — and every employee draws from it: answering customers on the website, picking up the phone, serving the team internally. Change a fact once, and every employee is up to date.

What makes them employees rather than chatbots is accountability. Each one carries a business objective and weighted KPIs, and every conversation is scored against that rubric — guardrail violations score negative, so the number can’t be gamed. They take real actions: booking appointments, capturing and routing leads, escalating to a human. And they hold a bright line we consider non-negotiable: a NeoMind employee never signs, commits, or makes a binding promise on your behalf. When a conversation reaches a judgement call, the right move is to escalate — and the scoring rewards exactly that.

Your knowledge stays yours. NeoMind learns from the sources you give it to serve you; it isn’t a way for your data to train someone else’s model.

The studio behind it

NeoMind isn’t a startup’s first guess at AI. It’s the productised form of ten years of building.

Neomeric was founded in Melbourne in 2015, and has worked one way since: design, build and operate — we run what we ship, we don’t hand over a deck and leave. That has meant 7+ products across 4+ industries — finance, logistics, services and manufacturing — including Aourix, a lead-to-cash ERP for manufacturers, and AuraFusen, an HR platform, alongside custom systems built and operated for clients.

A decade of that teaches you things no framework does. That a pilot must ship in weeks, because momentum is the project. That the smallest genuinely useful thing beats the impressive roadmap. That an AI you can’t measure is an AI you can’t trust with real work. And that the honest answer — escalate this to a human — is a feature, not a failure.

Every one of those lessons is built into NeoMind. The same way a NeoMind employee draws on one Brain, NeoMind draws on ours.
