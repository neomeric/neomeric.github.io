About

What a business knows shouldn’t depend on one person’s memory.

That conviction is why we built NeoMind — so the knowledge a business runs on can answer, act and be measured on its own, without walking out the door when someone does.

The why

Most businesses don’t have an AI problem. They have a knowledge problem.

Customers wait for answers that live in someone’s head. Staff repeat the same questions instead of doing the work. And when an experienced person leaves, the way things really work quietly leaves with them.

Customers wait too long for answersQuestions sit unanswered while staff are busy — or asleep.

Staff repeat the same questionsThe team answers the same things every week instead of doing the work.

Know-how walks out the doorWhen experienced people leave, the way things really work goes with them.

Owners can’t tell if AI is helpingThe few who do try AI have no way to see whether it’s actually working.

What we believe

The real problem AI should solve isn’t sounding smarter.

It’s making sure what a business knows survives, and works — without depending on any one person’s memory. A few convictions shape how we build toward that.

01

An AI you can’t measure is an AI you can’t trust with real work. If it can’t be scored, it can’t be relied on.

02

The honest answer — “let me escalate this to a human” — is a feature, not a failure. Knowing the limit is part of doing the job well.

03

We build and operate. We run what we ship, and ship the smallest thing that is genuinely useful — then earn the next step.

Our vision
Every company will have AI employees. The ones that win will be able to trust them — and measure them.
