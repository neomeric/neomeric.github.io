NeoMind vs Glean: enterprise search vs affordable AI teammates

Short answer: choose NeoMind if you’re an SMB or mid-market business that wants an affordable internal knowledge agent (Hugo) plus customer-facing web and phone agents — measurable, grounded, and live in an hour. Choose Glean if you’re a large enterprise that needs a permissions-aware knowledge graph unifying 100+ internal apps and you have the budget for it. Glean is a powerful enterprise Work-AI platform; NeoMind brings grounded internal and external AI agents to businesses that can’t justify an enterprise search deployment.

Where NeoMind wins

  • Affordability and accessibility. Hugo gives a small team a grounded internal helpdesk for staff questions — leave, expenses, policies, SOPs — on flat, predictable pricing, with no enterprise contract and no procurement cycle. Glean is built and priced for large enterprises.
  • Customer-facing too. NeoMind isn’t only internal — Simon handles your website and Maeve answers your phone, all from the same Brain. Glean is focused on internal enterprise knowledge.
  • You can measure it. Enterprise search shows you usage analytics. NeoMind gives you a per-employee scorecard: an objective, weighted KPIs, and every conversation scored by an AI judge, with guardrail violations scoring negative so the number can’t be gamed.
  • Self-serve and fast. Live in ~60 minutes, grounded and cited, with no rollout project.

Where Glean wins

Glean is a serious enterprise platform, and an honest comparison says so:

  • Enterprise breadth. Glean unifies a large estate of internal apps behind a permissions-aware knowledge graph — purpose-built for large, complex organisations.
  • Governance and scale. For thousands of employees with strict access controls, Glean’s permissions model and governance tooling are built for exactly that.
  • Deep enterprise integrations. If your knowledge is scattered across many enterprise systems, Glean’s connectors are designed for that sprawl.

Who should pick which

  • Pick NeoMind if you’re an SMB or mid-market business that wants an affordable internal knowledge agent plus customer-facing AI, fast and measurable.
  • Pick Glean if you’re a large enterprise needing deep, governed search across a sprawling internal app estate.

Glean is enterprise search for a large workforce. NeoMind is a small, measurable team of AI employees — internal and customer-facing — you can stand up today.
