What Simon does

A teammate who never misses a visitor.

Simon does the routine front-of-house work end-to-end — so your team only handles the conversations that genuinely need a human.

Answers, grounded and cited

Replies to buyer questions on any page using only your knowledge. Every answer can show its source, and when something isn’t known, Simon says so instead of guessing.

Qualifies & captures leads

Reads intent, asks the right follow-ups, and captures contact details when a visitor is ready — handing warm, qualified leads straight to your team.

Books appointments

Takes the next step a visitor wants — booking a call, quote or job directly into your calendar, around the clock, even after hours.

Escalates to a human

Knows where its job ends. When a conversation needs judgement or anything binding, Simon hands off cleanly — never signing or committing on your behalf.

Measurable

You can actually tell if Simon is doing a good job.

Simon isn’t a black-box chatbot. He carries a business objective you set — capture more qualified leads, book more consultations — and a handful of weighted KPIs that are scored on every single conversation by an AI judge: lead-capture rate, groundedness, appropriate escalation, resolution. Those roll up into a scorecard you read like a performance review, so you coach Simon the way you’d coach a person. See how measurement works →
