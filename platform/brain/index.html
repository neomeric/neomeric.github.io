Why it matters

Your knowledge,
indexed once and shared by all.

Most AI tools bolt onto a single channel and forget everything between conversations. The Brain is different: it's a durable, shared core that every employee learns from — so your knowledge compounds instead of scattering.

Upload & crawl

Drop in documents, spreadsheets, policies and FAQs, or point the Brain at your website and it crawls and indexes the pages. Add a text note in seconds.

Cited answers

Every answer is grounded in your indexed knowledge and shows the source it came from. When the Brain doesn't know, the employee says so — it doesn't invent.

Live re-index

Update a document and the Brain re-indexes it. The new information is available to your employees in seconds — no waiting on a training run.
