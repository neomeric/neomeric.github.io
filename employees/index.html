The honest limit

Employees do the routine work. Judgement stays human.

Every NeoMind employee does the routine work brilliantly and at volume — answering, capturing, booking, deflecting. But judgement calls and anything binding stay with your team. NeoMind never signs, commits, or makes a binding promise on your behalf. When a conversation reaches that line, the right move is to escalate to a human — and doing so raises the employee’s score rather than lowering it. That bright line is what makes a measurable AI employee safe to deploy.
