How it works

Four decisions and your employee is on the team.

You don’t write prompts or wire pipelines. You make four plain-language decisions, and NeoMind assembles a working, measurable employee on your Brain.

01

Persona

Give it a name, a role and a tone of voice — how it should sound and behave when it talks to people.

02

Skills

Pick what it can do — answer questions, capture leads, book, route, summarise — from the same skills the standard three use.

03

Knowledge scope

Choose which parts of your Brain it can read — so it’s an expert on exactly what it needs, and nothing it shouldn’t see.

04

Access policy

Set where it operates and what actions it’s allowed to take — you stay in control of the boundaries.

The result: a working AI employee on your shared Brain — consistent with the rest of the team, updated the instant your knowledge changes, and scored on every conversation.

Same guarantees

Custom, but never a loose cannon.

A custom agent is built the same way as a sales Simon or an HR Hugo — it’s just not derived from one of the three minds. It inherits everything that makes the standard agents trustworthy: it answers only from the knowledge you scope it to, with citations, and it’s measurable — its own objective and weighted KPIs, scored on every conversation by an AI judge and rolled into a scorecard.
